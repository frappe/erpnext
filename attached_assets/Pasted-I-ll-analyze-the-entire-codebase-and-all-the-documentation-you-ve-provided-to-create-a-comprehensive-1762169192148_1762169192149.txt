## **📊 Key Business Rules**

### **Zambian Tax Rules (2025)**
- **PAYE**: Progressive tax rates (0%, 20%, 30%, 37.5%)
- **NAPSA**: 5% employee + 5% employer (capped at K250/month each)
- **NHIMA**: 1% employee + 1% employer (capped at K500/month total, stops at age 60)
- **Skills Dev Levy**: 0.5% of gross for companies with turnover > K800,000
- **Personal Levy**: K7.5 per employee in March and June
- **VAT**: 16% standard rate
- **Service Charges** (Tourism): 10% of sales, added to gross, taxable

### **Compliance Deadlines**
- NAPSA: 10th of following month
- NHIMA: 10th of following month
- ZRA PAYE: 14th of following month
- VAT: 21st of following month
- Corporate Income Tax: Annual (within 6 months of financial year-end)

### **Inventory Rules**
- Reorder alerts when `quantity_on_hand <= reorder_level`
- Automatic stock updates on GRN posting
- Lot/batch expiry tracking
- Multi-location support
