ERIK ERP Developer brief – Finance & Accounting moduleBelow is a single, consolidated, developer-ready breakdown of everything we’ve discussed about Finance & Accounting for the ERIK ERP. It’s organized so engineers (backend, frontend, QA, devops) can pick up tasks quickly: features, data model, APIs, frontends (Python Streamlit + HTML/CSS/JS notes), business rules, integrations, compliance, testing, acceptance criteria, and a prioritized implementation plan.1 — High-level scope & goals Full general-ledger (GL) + AP + AR + fixed assets + cash/bank + multi-currency + VAT/Tax + payroll integration (payroll already defined in ERP) + invoicing (Smart Invoice-compliant) + reporting, reconciliation and audit trail. Support both compact journal entry format (amount once with both debit & credit accounts) and legacy line-by-line double-entry for compatibility. Designed as microservice API (FastAPI) with Python frontend (Streamlit) and optional HTML/CSS/JS frontends. Database: PostgreSQL (recommended) with transactional integrity. Audit-first: immutable audit trail, versioning of financial documents, user roles & permissions.2 — Modules / features (top-level)1. Chart of Accounts (COA) manager (multi-tenant, hierarchical)2. Journal Entries (compact & legacy)3. Ledger & Trial Balance4. Accounts Receivable (customer invoices, credit notes, receipts)5. Accounts Payable (supplier bills, credit notes, payments)6. Bank & Cash (bank accounts, bank statements, reconciliation engine)7. Fixed Assets (capex, depreciation schedules)8. Multi-currency engine (rates, effective-dates, revaluation)9. VAT / Sales Tax engine & VAT return reports10. Invoicing (Smart Invoice compliance, PDF/UBL exports)11. Payments (manual, bulk, payment runs; integrate with payment gateways)12. Intercompany transactions & eliminations13. Financial reporting (P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Custom reports)14. Period close / lock periods / opening balances15. Audit trail, approvals workflow, & role-based access controls16. Integrations: Payroll (internal), Banking (import MT940/CSV), OCR/GRN (procurement), external tax gateways17. Month / Year-end processes and automated adjustments (e.g., accruals, prepayments)18. API for data export (CSV, XLSX, JSON) and secure webhooks19. Smart Invoice Compliance revisions (modules 17,18,19,40,53 flagged for revision)20. Notifications & scheduled jobs (exchange rate updates, recurring invoices)3 — Core business rules & important specifics (from prior decisions) Compact journal entry structure: single amount field with debit and credit accounts in same entry. System must map this to double-entry postings internally for reporting and reconciliations. Payroll integration specifics: Payroll components included in gross salary (housing, lunch, transport, bonuses, gratuity), employer and employee NAPSA & NHIMA contributions shown separately; NHIMA = 1% of basic salary only (not gross). VAT / Smart Invoice: invoices must include required Smart Invoice metadata (supplier/customer tax IDs, invoice number, issue date, tax amounts, currency, exchange rates, invoice line-level data) and support both PDF & UBL/JSON export. Immutable audit trail: all financial docs (journals, invoices, payments) must have write-once records with separate corrective documents rather than editing in-place (store edits as new version records). Multi-currency: clients choose a home currency; they can add foreign currencies with configurable rate sources and effective dates. Show rates as “home currency per foreign currency” OR “foreign currency per home”, configurable per client. Reconciliation rules: payments and receipts must be matchable to invoices/bills; support partial matches, auto-suggest matches, manual adjustments, and unapplied receipts. Module revision list: Modules 17, 18, 19, 40, 53 must be revised for Smart Invoice compliance (per prior notes). Include umbrella heading in those revisions.4 — Data model (essential tables / entities)(Use PostgreSQL; include UUID PKs; timestamps; tenant_id for multi-tenancy)Primary tables (abbreviated)
