Create a Quotation

Path: Selling → Quotations → Quotation → New
Path: CRM → Opportunities → Opportunity → Select an opportinity → Click create at top right

Steps

  • Select Opportunity or Customer
  • Add Items and Quantity
  • Verify Rates, Discounts, and Taxes
  • Set Valid Till Date
  • Save and Submit
  • Click Send Email from the confirmation popup to share the quotation with the customer

Purpose

To provide a formal price offer to the customer and initiate the sales process.

Next Actions

  • Create a Sales Order from an accepted quotation
  • Track quotation status (Open, Ordered, Lost)
